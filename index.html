003  /  About

I build backends that move a lot of data — and tell you the truth about themselves.

I'm an Associate Software Engineer II at JP Morgan Chase in Glasgow, working on Broadsword, a data observability product. Most of my day is Java 21 and Spring Boot 3 microservices, Kafka topics, and the AWS infrastructure underneath them.

Before this: BMC Software on AMI Strobe, mainframe performance tooling for z/OS shops. Nextgen Healthcare on EHR features and FHIR interoperability. e-Zest on a Camunda-driven action engine. Capgemini on SAP PI integrations for General Electric. Six years of engineering across five companies, in India and the UK.

In 2024 I finished an MSc in Advanced Computer Science at the University of Strathclyde with Distinction — machine learning, search-based software engineering, and an Android health app built around Fogg's behaviour change model.

The work I care about is the unglamorous kind — finding where a system quietly stalls and making it stop doing that. Most of what I've shipped comes down to throughput, observability, and infrastructure other engineers can build on.
